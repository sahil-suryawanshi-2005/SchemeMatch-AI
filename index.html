Describe Your Circumstances

Describe your situation in simple words or complete your structured citizen profile—such as occupation, domicile, age, and annual family income.

Zero biometric upload needed
Step 02

Deterministic Gazette Crosscheck

Our rule engine compares your attributes against official Central and Maharashtra gazette notifications, evaluating conditions as Met, Unmet, or Action Needed.

Zero model hallucination
